# Accessibility Statement

[日本語](../ACCESSIBILITY.md) | English

Cerune is an experimental programming language developed by an individual for learning and research. The project primarily provides command-line tools (CLI) and text-based documentation.

We aim to make the project easier to use, explore, and contribute to by improving accessibility where feasible. However, **we do not guarantee compliance with a particular accessibility standard or usability in every environment**.

## Priorities

- Make CLI results and diagnostics understandable from text, without depending solely on color, symbols, or visual positioning.
- Use descriptive headings and link text in documentation, and provide alternative text or descriptions for meaningful images.
- Maintain text-based ways to use the project and report problems without requiring a mouse or visual interaction.
- Welcome reports and suggestions concerning accessibility barriers.

These are **goals**, not claims that every feature currently meets them or has been verified.

## Known limitations and untested areas

We have not conducted systematic testing with assistive technologies such as screen readers, braille displays, or voice control, or verified keyboard-only operation. Behavior may differ across operating systems, terminals, and text-rendering environments.

Barriers may remain in CLI output, error messages, documentation, and examples. We do not claim to have completed an accessibility audit or a WCAG conformance assessment.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

Please open a report through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Cerune-Lang/Cerune/issues/new/choose). If the available templates do not fit, a blank issue is also available. Including "Accessibility" in the title helps identify the report.

When possible, please include:

- The command, documentation, or output that was difficult to use, and what you expected instead.
- Steps to reproduce, relevant commands, or a link to the affected page.
- Your operating system, terminal, or assistive technology, to the extent you are comfortable sharing.

You do not need to disclose disability, medical, or other sensitive personal information to report a barrier.

If the report concerns a security vulnerability, please follow the [Security Policy](../SECURITY.md) and report it privately rather than opening a public issue.

## Maintenance and contributions

We review reports and consider improvements within the constraints of an individually maintained research project. We cannot promise a response deadline or a fix for every environment.

Contributions to improve the documentation or CLI are welcome. See the [Contributing Guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
